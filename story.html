The Adventure

Follow Rehema through ancient temples in a 2D adventure filled with platforming challenges. Run, jump, collect fruit, and avoid enemies and hazards across 10 story levels.

Along the way, uncover hidden histories connected to Africa and China through historical quiz challenges. The story explores heritage, identity, and perseverance.

Inspired by My Daughter

I created Rehema and the Lost Temple for my daughter, Rehema. This personal connection is at the heart of the game and its original story.

I am developing the game using the Godot Engine, combining my interest in technology with storytelling and game design.

Beyond the Story

Story Mode leads to a cinematic ending. For an ongoing challenge, Endless Mode lets players keep running and jumping to avoid obstacles and see how long they can survive.
