How to Play

Rehema runs forward automatically. Time your jumps to avoid enemies and hazards while collecting fruit.

Desktop and PC Controls
Action Control
Run Automatic forward movement.
Jump Press the Spacebar.
Double jump Press the Spacebar again while falling.
Collect items Touch an item to collect it automatically.
Avoid enemies and hazards Time your jumps to clear obstacles.
Answer a quiz Click one of the answer buttons.
Pause No dedicated pause control is available yet.

Game Modes

Story Mode

Explore 10 levels featuring ancient temples, collectibles, enemies, and historical quiz challenges connected to Africa and China.

Endless Mode

Rehema keeps running automatically. Control her jumps to avoid obstacles and see how long you can survive.

Tips for Your Adventure

  • Watch ahead for approaching enemies and hazards.
  • Time your jumps carefully when collecting fruit.
  • Use your second jump while falling.
  • Read each quiz question before choosing an answer.
