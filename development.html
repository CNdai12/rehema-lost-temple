Why I Started

My daughter, Rehema, is full of energy and loves being silly. I wanted to create a game that she could enjoy as her first gaming experience, without realizing at first that I made it for her.

That personal goal inspired Rehema and the Lost Temple and keeps me motivated as I develop it.

Building the Game

I am developing the game using the Godot Engine. The hardest part has been adding each level and coming up with different environment ideas. I want each area to feel different while still belonging to the same adventure.

Planned Improvements

  • Improve the controls and jumping experience.
  • Refine obstacles and their placement.
  • Develop more varied and interesting environments.

These are areas I want to keep improving as development continues.
